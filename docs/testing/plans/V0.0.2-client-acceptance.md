## Risk-based repetitions

Apply these counts to motion and interaction scenarios:

```text
Basic motion scenarios:
  normally 3 attempts
  stop after 3 consecutive unambiguous successes

Cooldown boundary:
  at least 5 consecutive inputs

Entity Hook rejection:
  1–2 representative attempts per configured target category

Anomaly, instability, or unclear reproducibility:
  hold conditions constant
  expand the affected scenario to at least 10 attempts
```

Artifact identity, configuration-presence, version/tier mismatch, unsafe-configuration, and
other fail-closed variants need one unambiguous result per distinct state. Repeat only when the
outcome is ambiguous or a bounded defect reproduction is required. A major failure observed once
is not diluted by later successes; preserve the reproduction evidence and classify its impact.

## Required boundary observations

### Range

- success within 16 blocks;
- boundary behavior near 16 blocks;
- safe rejection beyond 16 blocks;
- detach/break behavior when Player–Anchor distance approaches 32 blocks.

### Motion

- horizontal distance and controllability;
- vertical height gained;
- transition from `launch-speed=1.2` to pull;
- acceleration feel from `pull-acceleration=0.05`;
- speed cap behavior at `max-pull-speed=0.85`;
- input rejection during the 20-tick cooldown;
- successful reuse after cooldown.

### Elytra and Launchpad

- altitude/velocity/instability when deploying Elytra after Hook motion;
- velocity composition immediately after Launchpad;
- evidence when Hook is too weak to support Elytra transition;
- evidence when Hook over-shortcuts intended terrain.

### Display

Under the intended Resource Pack condition:

- record the Resource Pack identity/version and hash when available;
- held Hook model renders;
- launched Hook Head model renders;
- glowing/scale/offset do not obstruct operation.

If the intended Resource Pack is unavailable, mark the display checks `BLOCKED` and do not claim
complete Client Acceptance. Do not classify a missing-pack display result as movement balance.
Record display defects separately from movement balance.
