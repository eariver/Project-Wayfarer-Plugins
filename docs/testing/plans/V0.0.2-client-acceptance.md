fixed Client Test Candidate and a task-only Paper environment. Never use Project Runtime, Project
worlds, data, secrets, or players.

- Use task-only MariaDB/Redis and disposable worlds.

movement/range/display values from the reviewed LeafGrapple 1.0.2 standard tier; change only the

1. Confirm one canonical Growth Tool, owner use, non-owner/old-item rejection, reconnect, and no
   duplicate delivery.
2. Mine representative eligible and ineligible blocks; observe progress, evolution, and
   saturation-safe behavior without claiming exhaustive balance.
3. Observe broken state and repair preview/confirm/cancel, double-click, disconnect, and reconnect.
4. Verify death removes the managed tool without automatic return; exercise the paid Player reissue
   quote/confirm, old-item rejection, Pending Delivery, and free retry.
5. Verify Main V004/reissue recovery routes, sanitized output, and permission allow/deny behavior.

## Frontier representative checks

1. Enter exact `frontier_iris`; reject similar/nether/end worlds; confirm initial loadout,
   reconnect non-duplication, full-inventory Pending Delivery, and stale-identity guards.
2. With an approved safe LeafGrapple tier, observe canonical hook motion and verify durability and
   entity/player hooking restrictions.
3. Verify current English Navigation presentation and implemented actions; deferred Waystone is
   absent/non-actionable.
4. Place/use Launchpad and verify current-view direction, Sneak, cooldown, successful uses,
   auto-Elytra, restart/reconcile, and supported protection behavior.
5. Exercise Frontier permanent-item death redelivery, Safe Entry/respawn reconciliation, and
   free retry. Confirm Launchpad/Rocket are not treated as permanent free-redelivery items.
6. Verify permission leaves through LuckPerms, no OP dependency, sanitized output, exact portal
   boundary, and End Gateway as an observation only.

Stop once each representative result is unambiguous. Do not expand into stress, crash-matrix, or
unrelated feature exploration. A defect gets one bounded reproduction and an impact record.
